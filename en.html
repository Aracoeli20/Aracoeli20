Why stay longer

One room, three days of Puglia

Cassano is the exact spot where Puglia opens up in every direction without ever repacking. Park the car, take the keys to Lina or Mimì and use us as your base.

01

Matera & the wild Murgia

Morning in the UNESCO Sassi (40 min drive), afternoon in the Murgia Materana Park among rock churches and gorges. Back for dinner at a Cassano trattoria we'll recommend.

02

Woods, bread & stone towns

Morning walk in the Mercadante Forest (10 min), then Altamura for its DOP bread and Frederick II cathedral, and Gravina with its film-set aqueduct bridge. Sunset aperitivo over the Murgia.

03

Sea or trail, your choice

A day on the coast between Polignano a Mare and old Bari (40 min), or a taste of the Via Peuceta: we'll point you to the finest day section, with an easy return.

“Three nights are the right measure of the Murgia: the first day you discover it, the second you understand it, the third you never want to leave.”

Cammino Materano — Via Peuceta

A place made by a pilgrim, for pilgrims

Aracoeli 20 is an officially accredited stop on camminomaterano.it along the Via Peuceta, the route from Bari to Matera across the Alta Murgia. Cassano delle Murge is the end of Stage 2: here you stop, rest and set off again. Domenico, a pilgrim with many miles in his legs, is here to share advice and trail stories.

01Official stamp for the pilgrim credential
02Breakfast included, kitchen for dinner & departure
03Washing machine, drying rack & hairdryer
04Secure bike spot & tent slot available
05Up-to-date tips on the next stage, water sources & weather
Tappa 2 · ~22 km

Bitetto → Cassano delle Murge

Through olive groves and dry-stone walls, crossing the Mercadante Forest — the great green lung of the Murgia — to the old town of Cassano, steps from Aracoeli 20.

Tappa 3 · ~22 km

Cassano delle Murge → Santeramo in Colle

Set off into the Mesola Wood among downy oaks, fragno oaks and Aleppo pines, then rocky ridges, sinkholes, ravines and wheat fields to Santeramo.
