Close to the direct Cassano–Miulli bus stop

Our property is one of the closest to the stop of the direct connection between Cassano delle Murge and Miulli Hospital (SITA SUD line 7066, Via Vittorio Veneto stop). A valuable convenience for those without a car or who prefer not to drive after long, tiring days: you reach the hospital with no worries. Up-to-date timetables are on the SITA SUD Trasporti.
