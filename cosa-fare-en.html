Thrills and fun

Kartodromo della Murgia

🏁 A few minutes by car · Via per Santeramo km 3

Just a short hop from Aracoeli 20 is one of the most popular spots in the Bari area: the Murgia go-kart track, a floodlit circuit surrounded by greenery. Perfect for a different evening with friends, a birthday party or a family challenge — from beginners to motor enthusiasts. The current circuit is 700 metres, with an extension under way.

  • Floodlit track, open daily 9am–1pm and 3pm–9pm
  • Kart hire from €15 (10 min) up to €30 (30 min)
  • Birthday parties, corporate races and competitions
  • 4.6/5 on Google with over 600 reviews

Nature and sweetness

Educational beekeeping — Alveare da Favola

🐝 In Cassano delle Murge

A unique experience for all ages: discovering the world of bees up close with those who live it every day. Cassano has a long beekeeping tradition — the educational farm Alveare da Favola takes part in the Alta Murgia National Park's "Open Apiaries" initiative, with visits, teaching hives, honey tastings and workshops. A perfect activity for families with children and lovers of authentic nature.

  • Educational visits with expert beekeepers
  • Tastings of honey and hive products
  • Workshops for children and adults
  • Honeycomb honey, locally produced
In the open air

Mercadante Forest

🌲 About 7 km · 10 minutes by car

The great green lung of the Murgia: over a thousand hectares of equipped pine forest, planted in 1928 to reforest the plateau. It's the ideal place for a refreshing walk, a family picnic or a mountain-bike ride. Stage 2 of the Via Peuceta — the one that ends right in Cassano — crosses these woods, among oaks, fragno oaks and pines.

  • Equipped pine forest planted in 1928
  • Picnic areas and family-friendly trails
  • Hiking and MTB routes
  • Crossed by Stage 2 of the Via Peuceta
History and spirituality

Sanctuary of Santa Maria degli Angeli

⛪ A few minutes on foot from the walls

A short distance from the old town stands the Sanctuary-Convent of Santa Maria degli Angeli, an evocative convent set among caves and run by the Augustinian Fathers. Tradition tells of Marian apparitions and a sacred image frescoed on the rock. Entry is free: a place of peace and beauty, rich in artworks, steps from Aracoeli 20.

Nearby art cities

Half an hour's drive, centuries of history

Matera

41 km · ~45 min

The UNESCO Sassi, the rock churches and the Murgia Materana Park. One of the oldest cities in the world.

Altamura

~20 km · 25 min

The famous DOP bread, the cathedral built by Frederick II and the Man of Altamura. Capital of the Murgia.

Gravina in Puglia

~30 km · 35 min

The film-set aqueduct bridge, the ravines and the rock dwellings carved into the stone.

Bari

30 km · 30 min

Old Bari, the Basilica of San Nicola, the seafront and the international airport just 27 km away.

Polignano a Mare

40 km · 45 min

The cliffs over the Adriatic, the coves and the white town facing the sea. An unmissable seaside trip.
