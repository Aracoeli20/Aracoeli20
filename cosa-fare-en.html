Cassano delle Murge · Alta Murgia National Park

What to do in Cassano delle Murge,
from a single base

A forest ten minutes away, a rock sanctuary a twenty-five-minute walk from the door, Matera in three quarters of an hour and Bari in half an hour. From Aracoeli 20, in the old town, you can explore the Murgia without changing beds: here is what is worth seeing, and how long it takes to get there.

In the open air

Mercadante Forest

🌲 7-8 km · 10 minutes by car

The great green lung of the Murgia: over a thousand hectares of equipped pine forest, planted in 1928 to reforest the plateau. It's the ideal place for a refreshing walk, a family picnic or a mountain-bike ride. Stage 2 of the Via Peuceta — the one that ends right in Cassano — crosses these woods, among oaks, fragno oaks and pines.

  • Equipped pine forest planted in 1928
  • Picnic areas and family-friendly trails
  • Hiking and MTB routes
  • Crossed by Stage 2 of the Via Peuceta
History and spirituality

Sanctuary of Santa Maria degli Angeli

⛪ About 2 km · 25 minutes on foot, 5 by car

About two kilometres from the old town stands the Sanctuary-Convent of Santa Maria degli Angeli, an evocative convent set among caves and run by the Augustinian Fathers. Tradition tells of Marian apparitions and a sacred image frescoed on the rock. Entry is free: a place of peace and beauty, rich in artworks, a twenty-five-minute walk from Aracoeli 20.

Nature and sweetness

Educational beekeeping — Alveare da Favola

🐝 About 3 km · 5 minutes by car

A unique experience for all ages: discovering the world of bees up close with those who live it every day. Cassano has a long beekeeping tradition — the educational farm Alveare da Favola takes part in the Alta Murgia National Park's "Open Apiaries" initiative, with visits, teaching hives, honey tastings and workshops. A perfect activity for families with children and lovers of authentic nature. Before you go, check visit days and availability with the farm.

  • Educational visits with expert beekeepers
  • Tastings of honey and hive products
  • Workshops for children and adults
  • Honeycomb honey, locally produced
Thrills and fun

Kartodromo della Murgia

🏁 About 4 km · 6-8 minutes by car · SP236 towards Santeramo

On the provincial road to Santeramo is the Kartodromo della Murgia, a floodlit karting circuit surrounded by greenery and one of the most popular spots in the Bari area: evenings with friends, birthday parties, corporate races and competitions. Opening hours and rates change with the season, so we don't list them here: the circuit's own website has the current ones.

Coming from out of town for a race, or to support someone who is racing? With us you sleep a few minutes from the track and let yourself in with a code, at any hour.

  • Floodlit circuit surrounded by greenery
  • Birthday parties, corporate races and competitions
  • 6-8 minutes by car from Aracoeli 20

Nearby art cities

Matera

about 45 min by car

The UNESCO Sassi, the rock churches and the Murgia Materana Park. One of the oldest cities in the world.

Altamura

about 25 min by car

The famous DOP bread, the cathedral built by Frederick II and the Man of Altamura. Capital of the Murgia.

35-40 min by car

The film-set aqueduct bridge, the ravines and the rock dwellings carved into the stone.

Frequently asked questions

Before you set off

What can you do in Cassano delle Murge?

In and around Cassano delle Murge you can walk and picnic in Mercadante Forest, visit the rock Sanctuary of Santa Maria degli Angeli, discover an educational bee farm, try the track at the Kartodromo della Murgia, and reach Altamura, Gravina, Matera and Bari in 25 to 45 minutes.

Is Mercadante Forest suitable for families?

Yes. Mercadante Forest is an equipped pine forest 7-8 km from Cassano, about 10 minutes by car, with picnic areas and easy trails suitable for families, plus hiking and mountain-bike routes.

How far is Matera from Cassano delle Murge?

Matera is 41 km from Cassano delle Murge, about 45 minutes by car. Bari is about 30 km away, half an hour's drive; Altamura, the nearest art city, is about 25 minutes away.
